Most of my work is about getting dependable results out of models under a fixed budget. The budget changes: qubits, milliseconds, trainable parameters. The question does not.

## Research


**Quantum ML for cardiac diagnosis (Manipal, 2022 to 2023).** QuCardio benchmarks quantum kernel methods (QSVC, Pegasos QSVC) against a quanvolutional neural network for ECG-based cardiovascular disease detection. The QNN reached 97.31% accuracy. Funded by a $27,000 MeitY / AWS Quantum Computing Applications Lab grant. Published in IEEE Access, 60+ citations.

**Retinal imaging (IIT Kharagpur, summer 2023).** With Dr. Subhamoy Mandal at the Medical Informatics Lab: a cross-attention vision transformer that fuses thermal and OCT structural maps to identify age-related macular degeneration.

**Earlier work (Manipal).** Attention and residual U-Nets for retinal vessel segmentation, with Diffie-Hellman + AES-256 encryption so patient images stay private (EyeEncrypt, with Dr. Srikanth Prabhu). BERT and XLNet classifiers for vaccine misinformation at 94.84% accuracy, plus a Reddit user-association graph with community detection to find high-leverage accounts (with Dr. Nisha P. Shetty).
